## Security Policy

### Supported Versions

Security fixes are generally applied to:

- The **main** branch (latest development).
- Any actively deployed production versions operated by the project owner.

If you are running a fork or a significantly modified version, you are responsible for evaluating and backporting relevant fixes.

---

### Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in **Bricks Backend**, **please do not open a public issue**.

Instead, report it privately:

- **Email**: `rjsharmase@gmail.com`

Please include:

- A **description of the vulnerability** and potential impact.
- **Steps to reproduce** or proof of concept.
- Any relevant **logs, configuration details, or environment information** (redact secrets and personal data).

We aim to:

- Acknowledge your report as soon as reasonably possible.
- Investigate and assess the impact.
- Work on a fix and plan coordinated disclosure where needed.

---

### Responsible Disclosure

We kindly ask that you:

- Give us a **reasonable amount of time** to investigate and deploy a fix before publicly disclosing details.
- Avoid actions that could:
  - Interrupt service,
  - Access or modify data that does not belong to you,
  - Violate privacy or applicable laws.

We commit to:

- Treat security reports **seriously and confidentially**.
- Keep you informed of the progress where appropriate.
- Credit you in release notes or advisories, if you wish and if applicable.

---

### Out of Scope

The following are generally out of scope for this security policy:

- Reports about **older, unsupported versions** of the project.
- **Denial-of-service** attacks that require unrealistic conditions or are purely theoretical.
- Issues requiring **physical access** to servers or devices.
- Vulnerabilities in **third‑party dependencies** where the issue must be fixed upstream (though we still appreciate reports that help us track and update).

---

Thank you for helping keep **Bricks Backend** and its users secure.
